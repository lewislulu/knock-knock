The first public release of **knock-knock**, a native macOS calendar reminder with a playful side.

### English

- Five reminder styles: classic, pixel cat, banner, girl knocking, and a comic FBI-style visitor.
- Five original sound effects, volume controls, animation, and optional repeat nudges.
- English, Simplified Chinese, and Japanese interfaces.
- Calendar filters, advance notice, snooze, pause, meeting links, and launch at login.
- New door-and-knocks logo, bilingual documentation, and MIT-licensed source and artwork.
- Local calendar processing. Published images use fictional demo events and contain no personal calendars.

**Download:** `knock-knock-v1.0.0-macos-arm64.dmg`.
Requires **macOS 14+ and Apple Silicon**. Intel, Windows, and Linux are not supported by this release.

Open the DMG and drag the app into Applications. This build is **ad-hoc signed and not notarized**; macOS may require **System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway** after you verify the download source. Managed Macs may block it. Grant Calendar access after launch, and keep the app running for reminders.

`SHA256SUMS` provides the download checksum. In the download folder, run `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS`.
